# Saved-consumer usefulness review

The maintainer approved reviewing retained consumer results, addressing the most
useful demonstrated gap, then pausing for a separate matter **before 0.15.0
consolidation**. This checkpoint completes the bounded technical reading and
prepares the priority source-review work. Complete primary methods and human
scientific acceptance remain open.

## What was checked

An inert private reader using the current editable SDK verifies original saved
structural, activity and comparative artifacts against their retained receipts.
It checks original file and retrieval-archive hashes, sealed card snapshots and
pins, relationship/assertion membership, original workflow records and structural
inventory links. All tracked pilot files and the inspected original inputs/stores
remain unchanged.

Source transport, source resolution, SDK scientific views and fresh credit are
forbidden during this reading. Historical rules and results retain their original
producer; they are not silently recalculated under current rules. Notebook source
is inspected without executing cells. Plans and historical notebook displays
without their complete saved baseline are not counted as accepted current runs.
The public clinical demonstration retains its separate public-fixture scope.

Detailed inventories, scientific values, notebook content, source identifiers,
exact pins, hashes and new consumer aggregates remain private. No new scientific
provider request, curation, scientific interpretation or project Evidence is
produced. This is agent technical inspection, not scientist acceptance.

## Finding and chosen improvement

The inspected saved-reading scope exposes no new SDK failure. Existing snapshots,
source support and original assay qualifiers are accessible with the current
implementation. The earlier public dossier improvements already expose native
assay/target/document context; another wrapper would not supply missing primary
experimental methods.

The useful immediate improvement is a private **assay review queue**. It retains
original measurement qualifiers and exact pinned relationship/SourceAssertion
references, organized by literal source, assay, target and document context. This
named grouping is a navigation aid, not assay identity, protocol equivalence,
molecule ranking or a count of independent experiments. Complete assertions stay
in their original sealed cards rather than being duplicated for every row.

Each queue entry leaves review pending for construct/mutations, substrate and
concentrations, buffer/pH/temperature, incubation versus preincubation, readout and
interference controls, and measurement method. Free descriptions are retained,
not parsed into accepted protocols. The queue does not establish that a condition
is absent from a source merely because a mapped field is missing.

The [primary-method access review](primary_assay_methods_review.md) still owns the
document identity/access findings. Actual methods must be obtained and verified
before protocol comparability is accepted. This review does not resolve that
access need. Human model selection, substantive curation and consumer-owned
interpretation remain with their respective owners.

## Qualification and scheduling boundary

Private review and queue artifacts retain their owner and a review trigger after
source/scientist review or the next substantial release audit. They stay outside
Git; originals are preserved. Applicable documentation, link, governance and
source-identity checks cover this checkpoint. No SDK, tests, fixtures, schemas,
dependencies or workflows change, and no new code/installed-artifact checkpoint
is claimed. The [qualified clinical source receipt](clinical_bibliography_journey_checkpoint.json)
retains its original scope.

Pause here for the maintainer's separate matter. Do not start 0.15.0 consolidation,
candidate selection, schema freezing, staging builds or publication before that
matter is addressed and the maintainer resumes this route. The release remains a
[proposal](release_consolidation_0150.md). Keep #132, #92 and coordinating #112 open.
